First time? How to connect
  1. Find the OBD port: a trapezoid-shaped plug under the dashboard on the driver's side, usually near your left knee or above the pedals.
  2. Push the adapter in all the way. Its light should turn on.
  3. Turn the key or press Start so the dashboard lights come on. Start the engine for the best results.
  4. Tap Scan car and pick the adapter (e.g. "VEEPEAK" or "OBDII") from the list. Don't pair it in your phone's Bluetooth settings first.
